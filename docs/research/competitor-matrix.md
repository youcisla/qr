# Competitor matrix: QR platforms (Yopem QR rebuild)

Research date: **2026-10-25**. Scope: 11 products in three groups: QR-first generators (QRCode
Monkey, QRCG by Bitly, Uniqode, QR TIGER, Hovercode, Scanova, qr.io), link-platform QR (Bitly),
campaign QR (Flowcode), and design-tool generators (Adobe Express, Canva).

**Evidence policy.** Every row cites shipped artifacts: product/help pages, public API docs, or data
shipped in client HTML (inline JSON/i18n dictionaries). "Observed" = the claim is visible in the
cited artifact. "Inferred" = a reasonable deduction from observed capabilities. Client JS bundles
could not be retrieved with this toolchain (the fetch tool rejects `application/javascript`
responses and public proxies returned 401/522), so bundle-level strings are taken from JSON that
ships inside HTML pages (see design-taxonomy.md for exact quoted strings and URLs). Pricing is as
displayed on the captured date; treat as snapshot, not contract.

## 4. Patterns the market validates (steal these)

1. **The 3-layer style model is universal**: operators/pattern, then eyes split into frame + ball,
   then background. Every serious customizer exposes this decomposition (QRCode Monkey
   `body`/`eye`/`eyeBall`; Uniqode Eyes tab with ball and frame; Hovercode `pattern` + `eye_style`;
   QR TIGER Pattern + Eyes). Our `QrDesign` (dotStyle, eyeFrameStyle, eyeBallStyle) already matches
   the converged model.
2. **Templates are the on-ramp**: prebuilt gallery + "save as template" is table stakes (QRCG,
   Uniqode, QR TIGER, Hovercode 13+ named templates, Scanova themes). Shipping >=18 presets (studio
   slice) is competitive, not gold-plating.
3. **Frames with CTA text sell branding**: QRCG, Uniqode, Flowcode, Scanova, QR TIGER, Hovercode all
   ship frames with "SCAN ME"-style captions and custom text (QR TIGER: "Frame text (limited to 15
   characters)"; Hovercode frames expose `can_have_text`, `text_max_length` 16/24). Our
   `QrFrameOptions` matches, but should cap label length (~15-24 chars) like the market.
4. **Scannability guardrails are a product feature, not a warning footnote**: Uniqode publishes a
   40% contrast rule and "always scan on a mobile device"; QRCG tells users to test "both before and
   after publishing, and at the size it will be published to"; QR TIGER ships "Always scan to test
   that your QR code works" and "QR code design that may not work on some older Android cameras";
   Flowcode's auto-design refuses risky color combinations/error rates. Our `ScanWarning` panel is
   directly aligned — make it loud (error/warning/info) and show on export.
5. **Dynamic = short link + analytics, and "unique" is defined as a device-level identifier**, not a
   session: QRCG "unique scans refer to the specific device used for the scan"; Uniqode "distinct
   devices"; Flowcode "unique individuals" filters repeat scans by the same person; Scanova keeps a
   public "how scans are counted" page with bot filtering. Definition transparency is a
   differentiator.
6. **Spreadsheet export is expected**: CSV (QRCG, Uniqode Core+, Hovercode Business, Flowcode) and
   even CSV+Excel with raw scan-level rows and scheduled email reports (Scanova). Our CSV export
   slice should include raw scan events, not just an aggregated summary.
7. **The export matrix converges on PNG + SVG + PDF + EPS** (QRCode Monkey, Uniqode, QR TIGER,
   Scanova vectors; Adobe adds JPEG/PDF). SVG/PDF are table stakes for print; EPS is the
   differentiator only QRCode Monkey/Uniqode/QR TIGER ship. We should ship SVG/PNG/JPEG/PDF and
   consider EPS later (P2).

## 5. Gaps and opportunities for Yopem QR

- **Open-source + self-host is the positioning no listed competitor occupies** (all are SaaS; only
  QRCode Monkey is free-as-in-beer but closed). Free vector export, unlimited dynamic codes, and
  design JSON ownership are the wedge.
- **Design portability is absent**: no competitor lets you export/import the design definition
  itself. Hovercode exposes template IDs and a design object via API; QRCode Monkey exposes config
  JSON. A first-class, documented `QrDesign` JSON (versioned) is a genuine differentiator for teams
  and agencies.
- **Quiet-zone and print-safety controls are under-exposed**: no dedicated quiet-zone slider was
  observed in any customizer UI; guidance is prose ("increase minimum printing size" at high EC —
  QRCG). A quiet-zone slider + print-size calculator (min cm at scan distance) is cheap and useful.
- **Password/expiry/A-B are premium everywhere** (QR TIGER, Scanova, Uniqode Smart Rules, Bitly
  routing). Shipping them free in an open-source tool is a clear differentiator.
- **Scanner-side is not a generator feature**: none of the generators ships a structured-result
  scanner that parses vCard/WiFi/event payloads into actions; our scanner + `parseQrPayload`
  (codec-dev slice) is differentiated and feeds the analytics story ("what got scanned").
- **Analytics definitions are inconsistent and opaque**: different "unique" definitions and varied
  retention windows. Publishing our exact visitor-hash/unique definition + privacy posture (IP
  hashed with salt) is a trust differentiator in the EU context.
- **A/B testing is thin**: only Bitly dynamic routing and Uniqode Smart Rules approximate it; a
  weight-based A/B destination split (plus QR-level tagging) would be ahead of the market (P2).

## 6. What we deliberately do not chase

- Flowpage/Linkpage builders, form builders, and digital business card profile pages (Uniqode,
  Flowcode, Scanova, Hovercode): large product surfaces with little QR-engine leverage; not needed
  for a QR manager.
- AI-generated QR art (Scanova AI-Generated Design beta): image-generation dependency and
  scanability risk; explicitly out of scope.
- GS1 Digital Link / 2D barcodes (Bitly, QR TIGER, Scanova, Hovercode): regulated packaging domain,
  separate data model; P3 at most.
- Enterprise SSO/SAML, SCIM, audit logs: enterprise go-to-market, no product-learning value at this
  stage.
